\subsection{Q1(d) Two constraints beyond keys/foreign keys alone}\label{tutorial1_answers-q1d-two-constraints-beyond-keysforeign-keys-alone}

\textbf{1. Every owner has at least one pet.}

\StudyFormula{\forall o\,\in \,\mathsf{Owner},\,\exists p\,\in \,\mathsf{Pet}:\,p.\mathsf{ownerID}\,=\,o.\mathsf{ownerID}}

Mechanism: global assertion / transaction-end constraint trigger.

\textbf{2. Treatment numbers respect chronological order within each pet.}

\StudyFormula{\forall t,u\,\in \,\mathsf{Treatment}:}
\StudyFormula{(t.\mathsf{ownerID},t.\mathsf{petNo})\,=\,(u.\mathsf{ownerID},u.\mathsf{petNo})}
\StudyFormula{\land \,t.\mathsf{treatmentNo}\,<\,u.\mathsf{treatmentNo}}
\StudyFormula{\Longrightarrow \,t.\mathsf{date}\,\le \,u.\mathsf{date}}

Mechanism: assertion / trigger checking affected histories on INSERT/UPDATE.

\emph{Same-clinic management in (c) also needs an extra check; alternatively, declare UNIQUE(empID,\allowbreak{}clinicID) on Veterinarian and add FK Clinic(managerID,clinicID) → Veterinarian(empID,clinicID).}
